    How this was built

    One request typed during the meeting started it. A day of follow-up requests, testing and AI review finished it.

    The request

    Build me a 3D model of America 250 event over the 4th of July in DC with lots of event scenarios, scans of places in DC of the event, etc. Interview answers: AI as a thinking partner + "my team could do this" · ready by the next break · live exercise framing · Ask-the-AI, after-action report, aerial stills · make it interactive.

    The receipt

    30 minfrom the request to a first working link. A rough first version.
    +1 dayof follow-up requests, testing and AI review before it looked like this
    ~1,800lines of code, all written by AI
    9scripted injects. Any what-if the room invents runs live inside Claude.

    What the AI did

    • Modeled the Mall from public geography: 20+ landmarks, ~800 city blocks of buildings, the Potomac, Metro stations, a crowd model of ~16,000 tracked points.
    • Wrote a 9-inject master scenario events list (MSEL) spanning ESF-1, 6, 8, 9, 12, 13 and 15, each with a decision point, consequences and guidance.
    • Wired in an AI advisor that answers questions with the current exercise picture, critiques each decision, invents new injects on request, and drafts the after-action report. It runs live when this page is opened in Claude; on this public copy it falls back to built-in guidance.
    • AI reviewers did realism passes and tested the page on desktop and phone. No emergency manager or agency has reviewed it. Treat it as a first draft for your planners, not a validated MSEL.
    • Added over the next day, on request: a phone layout, What-if dials for planning assumptions, Your City, which drafts a six-inject exercise for any city, and the Ad Astra AI look. Each was tested on a phone-sized screen before going live.
